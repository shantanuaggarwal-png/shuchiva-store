About Shuchiva Essentials

Our Story

Shuchiva Essentials is a dedicated hygiene and cleaning brand by Nand Lal Ram Charan Dass, proudly based in Dehradun. We specialize in manufacturing premium, high-performance microfiber and coral fleece towels designed for both delicate personal care and heavy-duty cleaning.

Engineered for Quality

Every product in our catalog is built with material science in mind. Our signature cloths are crafted from a highly effective blend of 80% Polyester and 20% Polyamide, ensuring exceptional durability and softness. We offer specialized fabric weights and finishes to suit exact needs, including:

  • 400 GSM Multipurpose Cloths: Featuring a weft silky finish for soft, everyday absorbency.
  • 550 GSM Coral Fleece: Designed with edgeless ultrasonic cuts for scratch-free, quick-drying performance.
  • 800 GSM Dual-Sided Coral Fleece: High-density, short-pile construction for maximum liquid retention and heavy-duty tasks.

Versatility for Every Routine

We structure our product lines to serve multiple aspects of your daily life:

  • Personal Hygiene: Ultra-soft textures ideal for skin care routines, bath towels, and sensitive baby cloths.
  • Automobile Detailing: Thick, scratch-free fabrics engineered specifically for safe car and bike cleaning without damaging paint or clear coats.
  • Home & Utility: Reliable, multipurpose towels perfect for kitchen spaces, optical care, and general household maintenance.

We remain committed to delivering professional-grade utility, vibrant multi-color designs, and lasting comfort directly to your home.
